# 04 — User Personas and Journeys

**Status:** Draft. These personas are assumptions to be validated with the owner. They are archetypes, not research findings.

## Personas

**P1 — Technical recruiter (time-poor).** Arrives through a shared link, scans for about 60 seconds, and needs relevance, credibility signals and a CV download.

**P2 — Hiring manager or technical lead.** Wants depth: problem, approach, the owner's role and evidence such as repos, papers and demos.

**P3 — Academic or research collaborator.** Looks for publications, methods and research interests.

**P4 — Community or nonprofit partner.** Interested in volunteering, community impact and values.

**P5 — Owner (admin, future).** Maintains content once and assembles tailored views and CVs quickly.

## Key journeys

**J1 — Recruiter via shared link (P1).** Sheila sends a reusable link, such as a discipline page (`/portfolio/cybersecurity`) or a focus view (`/for/security-roles`). The page opens with a tailored intro and the relevant work, and the recruiter downloads the matching ATS-safe CV. Success means the recruiter reaches the CV in no more than two interactions.

**J2 — Discipline exploration (P2, P3).** Home, then a discipline, then a project, then a related publication or project. Success means related items are always linked and there are no dead ends.

**J3 — Credibility check (P2).** On a project page the reader sees the owner's role and links to evidence, then checks a certification's verification link.

**J4 — Owner maintains a focus view (P5).** Sheila creates a focus view once (in Supabase Studio, or later in the admin dashboard), picks and orders the items, and reuses the same link for every relevant recruiter.

**J6 — Visitor gets in touch.** On the Contact page the visitor either uses the email link or submits the form. They see a clear confirmation, and Sheila receives an email notification.

**J7 — Reader finds Sheila's writing.** From Writing or a discipline page, the reader opens an article and reads it on the site or follows the link to the original on Medium or dev.to.

**J5 — Unpublished or removed page.** The visitor sees a polite "not available" page that links to the portfolio. It must not return a 500 error or reveal unpublished content.
